Opening Word documents is much more complete:

- **Numbering like Word:** outline numbers (1, 1.1, 1.1.1, (a), (i)) keep their exact numbers and line up with a hanging indent. Lists that continue after a break keep counting.
- **Logos in headers and footers:** pictures in a Word header or footer now appear on every page, print, and save back. Insert → Header lets you add, move (left / centre / right), resize or remove a header or footer picture.
- **Tab leaders:** "Name: ........" and "Class: ______" lines show their dots and lines and stop at the margin.
- **Text boxes and wrapped pictures:** Word text boxes open as DocDoc text boxes with their border and fill. Pictures with text wrapped around them stay beside the text, and move to the next page together with their paragraph.
- **Equations** show as proper maths and save to Word as pictures.
- **Footnotes and endnotes** appear under a short line at the end, with their numbers.
- **Also kept:** merged table cells, tracked changes (shown as the final text), double underline, small caps, highlight, checkboxes and page breaks.
- **Saving back to Word keeps more:** bold titles, small caps, hanging indents, tab leaders, table borders, wrapped pictures and equations now survive a save.
- Fixed: Word files saved by DocDoc put the page settings in the order Word expects.
